About

Quality is a habit, not an audit.

I work at the point where a customer's problem meets a company's process — finding out why something went wrong, fixing it so it doesn't happen again, and coaching the team that has to live with the fix.

Over 8+ years I've moved from the shop floor to B2B sales to customer experience leadership, which means I've seen service failures from every angle: as a production supervisor accountable for output, as a sales engineer accountable for a client relationship, and now as a team leader accountable for the people who answer the phone. That range is what I bring to root-cause analysis — I don't just log the complaint, I trace it back through the process that produced it.
